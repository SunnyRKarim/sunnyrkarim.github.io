Research Agenda

I am currently a Ph.D. Candidate in Economics at Carleton University, specializing in econometrics and health economics. My research focuses on developing methods for policy evaluation to improve estimation and inference, with an emphasis on difference-in-differences approaches.

I am particularly interested in extending these methods when standard assumptions are difficult to justify and applying them to important empirical questions. I am also interested in applications in health and labour economics.

Through a combination of theoretical work and applied research, I aim to make empirical studies more credible.

Research Interests

Econometrics

Difference-in-differences, causal inference, estimation, and inference.

Applied Microeconomics

Health economics, labour economics, and policy evaluation.
